# Drawing QA

Check title blocks/revisions; references and callouts; grids/levels/north/scales/units; key and conflicting dimensions; room data; door/window tags and schedules; materials/legends/notes; plan/elevation/section consistency; missing/duplicate sheets; obvious coordination risks; and print/readability.

Finding format:
`ID | file/sheet | location | category | observation | evidence | impact | confidence | next action`

Severity: Blocker prevents reliable issue/use; Major implies likely coordination/compliance/construction impact; Minor is documentation/readability.

Do not label a design noncompliant without verified applicable rules.
